Grainday

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: Grainday does not collect any data. Your photos and videos are processed and stored on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

This policy explains how Grainday (the "App"), a retro camera app for iPhone made by dreamcraft.io ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

Every photo and video you take or import is processed on your iPhone and kept in the App's private storage. We do not run servers for the App, and it never uploads your photos, videos or any other content to us or anyone else. The App works without an internet connection.

If you turn on Keep clean originals, the App also keeps an unfiltered copy of each new photo, so you can develop it again later. These originals are stored only on your device, alongside your shots.

Photos the App saves include the date they were taken and the name of the Grainday camera used. The App does not record your location.

Permissions

The App asks only for what it needs, and only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera: to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone: only while you record a video, to capture its sound. You can decline and record silent video.
  • Photos (add only): to save your shots to your Camera Roll. The App cannot see or read your photo library. When you import a photo, you choose it in the system photo picker, and only that photo is given to the App.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you when photos with a develop delay are ready. These reminders are scheduled on your device.

The optional on-screen level uses your iPhone's motion sensors while the camera is open. This happens on your device and nothing is stored or sent.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App only learns which products you own, so it can unlock cameras. We never receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details. To check and restore purchases, the App communicates with Apple's App Store; that is the only network connection it makes.

Siri and Shortcuts

The App offers shortcuts such as "Open Camera" and "Open Roll". Requests you make through Siri are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The App only receives the instruction to open a screen; it never takes a photo on its own.

iPhone Duo outer display

On iPhone Duo you can show the person you're photographing a live preview on the outer display. The preview is shown only on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not create a profile of you. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

Deleting your data

You are always in control. Delete individual shots in the App's roll, or delete the App to remove everything it stored, including any clean originals. Copies you saved to your Camera Roll are managed in the Photos app and are not affected by deleting Grainday.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Everything the App stores is on your device and under your control. If you have a question about your privacy rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the details below.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a change ever means the App would collect data, we will say so clearly in the App before it happens.
