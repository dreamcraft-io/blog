Free and paid cameras

Six cameras are free: Kiosk 400, Sunroom 160, Pocket '03, Noir 400, Tape '92 and Snapshot 640. Every other camera can be tried in the viewfinder and unlocked in one of three ways:

  • Single camera: a one-time purchase that unlocks that camera.
  • Glytt Pro: a monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks every camera, including new ones added in updates.
  • Lifetime: a one-time purchase that unlocks every current and future camera.

Restore purchases

Open Glytt, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Purchases work on every iPhone signed in to that account.

Cancel or change a subscription

Glytt Pro renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can cancel or switch between monthly and yearly in Glytt's Settings > Manage Subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com, since payments are handled by Apple.

Camera, microphone and Photos access

  • If the viewfinder stays dark, allow camera access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.
  • Videos without sound: allow the microphone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Glytt records silent video when it's off.
  • Shots not appearing in your Camera Roll: allow Glytt to add photos in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. Glytt can only add photos; it cannot see your library.

Where your photos are

Shots are kept in Glytt's Roll on your iPhone and saved to your Camera Roll. Glytt has no accounts or servers, so nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes its Roll, but copies in your Camera Roll stay. Read the full Privacy Policy.
